# SierraZim website — how to edit your own site

This guide is for the SierraZim team. No technical knowledge needed.
Everything on the website that changes over time — photos, programmes, past
projects, phone numbers, staff — you can edit yourself, without a developer.

---

## 1. Logging in

Go to **your website address followed by `/admin`**:

```
https://www.sierrazim.com/admin
```

Sign in with the email and password you were given. If you forget the password,
use "Forgot password" on that screen.

> **Tip:** bookmark this page. It is the control panel for the whole site.

---

## 2. The one idea you need to understand

The control panel has two kinds of things in the left sidebar:

| | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Pages** | A fixed page on the site. You edit the **words and photos** on it. You cannot add a new page. | Home Page, About Page, Contact Page |
| **Content** | **Lists** of things. You can **add**, **edit**, **reorder** and **delete** items. | Gallery, Programmes, Past Projects, Clients |

So: if you want to change a *heading*, look under **Pages**.
If you want to *add another photo or another course*, look under **Content**.

---

## 3. The job you'll do most: adding gallery photos

**Content → Gallery**

Each gallery photo is one item. To add one:

1. Click **Gallery**, then **Create new**.
2. **Caption** — a short line shown under the photo, e.g. *"Excavator training — Bo"*.
3. **Alt text** — describe the photo in words. This is for blind visitors and
   for Google. Just write what is happening: *"Trainee operating an excavator on
   the practice yard."*
4. **Category** — pick one. This decides which **filter tab** the photo appears
   under on the gallery page:
   - Classroom & Theory
   - Light Vehicle
   - Heavy Equipment
   - Simulator
   - Certification
   - Projects & Events
   - Testimonials
5. **Media type** — leave as **Image** (choose *Video* only for a video).
6. **Image** — click, then **Upload** and choose the photo from your computer.
7. **Order** — a number. Lower numbers appear first. Leave `0` if you don't mind.
8. **Save**.

To **remove** a photo: open it and click **Delete**. To **hide** it temporarily,
just delete it — the original file stays in Media and you can re-add it later.

### Photo advice

- **Landscape (wide) photos look best.** Very tall photos get cropped.
- Upload the **original** photo from the phone or camera — the site shrinks and
  optimises it automatically. Don't worry about file size.
- **Don't upload near-identical shots.** If you took 10 photos of the same
  moment, pick the best one. Ten similar photos make the gallery look padded;
  one good photo looks professional.
- Aim for **variety** — different courses, different machines, different people.

### Adding a video

Same as above, but set **Media type** to *Video*, set **Provider** to *File
(MP4)* and paste the video address, or use *YouTube* / *Vimeo* and paste just
the video ID. Add a **Poster** image — this is the still picture shown before
someone presses play.

## 4. Training programmes

**Content → Programmes**

Each course you offer is one item: title, short description, full description,
who it's for, format, certification, a photo, and a list of features.

- **Adding a programme here automatically adds it to the Training page** and to
  the list on the home page. The heading that says *"Seven programmes"* counts
  them for you — it will say *"Eight programmes"* the moment you add one. You
  don't have to change anything else.
- **Order** controls the position in the list.

---

## 5. Past projects (your portfolio)

**Content → Case Studies**

This is your track record — the work you show off. Each one has the client,
sector, location, year, what you delivered, the outcomes, and photos.

- Tick **Featured** to push one to the top of the Portfolio page.
- The **Gallery** field on a case study holds the photos for that specific job.

**Content → Clients** is the separate list of company names shown as a band of
logos/names. **Content → Sectors** groups your work by industry (mining,
construction, etc.).

---

## 6. Contact details, staff and company info

**Pages → Site Settings**

This is the single place for information that appears **everywhere** on the site
(header, footer, contact page, WhatsApp button):

- Company name, tagline, description
- **Email, primary phone, secondary phone, WhatsApp number**
- City, region, country, opening hours
- **Leadership** — your team. Each person has a name, a role and a photo.
  Add or remove people here and the About page updates itself.

> Change a phone number here **once** and it changes on every page. You never
> need to hunt through the site.

---

## 7. Editing the words on a page

**Pages → Home Page / About Page / Contact Page / …**

Each page is split into **tabs** (Hero, Sections, Statement & stats) so you can
find things. Field names describe where the text appears:

- **Hero** = the big banner at the very top of the page.
- **Eyebrow** = the small line of text *above* a heading.
- **Heading** = the large title.
- **Intro** = the paragraph under the heading.
- **Green word / accent** = the word shown in green for emphasis.
- **Closing CTA Band** = the big two-line call-to-action block near the
  bottom of Home, About, Training, Work, Gallery and Sectors pages
  ("Line 1" / "Line 2", an optional intro line, the button text, and
  whether the second button calls, WhatsApps, or is left off).

**If you leave a field empty, the site falls back to the original wording.** So
you cannot break a page by clearing a box — it just returns to the default.

---

## 8. Saving and seeing your changes

1. Click **Save**.
2. Open the live site and refresh.

Changes to **Gallery, Programmes, Case Studies and Site Settings** appear on the
live site shortly after saving. If you don't see a change immediately, wait a
minute and do a hard refresh (**Ctrl+Shift+R**, or **Cmd+Shift+R** on a Mac).

---

## 9. A few things worth knowing

- **Always fill in Alt text** on photos. It's how blind visitors and Google
  understand your images. It takes five seconds and helps you rank.
- **Media** in the sidebar is the storage cupboard for every file ever uploaded.
  You normally don't go there directly — you upload from wherever you need the
  photo. Deleting something from Media that is still used on a page will leave a
  gap, so delete from the page/gallery item instead.
- **Users** is for adding another staff account to the control panel. Give
  people their own login rather than sharing one.
- **Don't panic.** Nothing you do in the control panel can break the website's
  design or layout. The worst case is wrong words or a missing photo, and you
  can always change it back.
